My Publications
I have published my research in different journals

  • 2023 - Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) for Vapour-Liquid-Liquid Equilibrium (VLLE) Predictions in N-Octane/Water Blends
    Application of Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) to predict phase behavior of n-octane/water blends
  • 2022 - Vapour–liquid–liquid and vapour–liquid equilibrium of paraffinic aromatic synthetic naphtha/water blends: Prediction of the number of phases
    Application of thermodynamic models (NRTL, UNIQUAC, and PC-SAFT) and Machine Learning (KNN, SVM) to predict phase behavior of PASN/water blends. Student's t-test was used to was employed to statistically differentiate experimental results between two-phase and three-phase regions.
  • 2022 - A Machine Learning Approach for Phase-Split Calculations in n-Octane/Water and PASN/Water Systems
    Application of Machine Learning (linear regression, KNN, SVM, and decision tree regressor (DTR)) to predict phase behavior of n-octane/water and PASN/water blends
  • 2021 - Thermodynamics and Machine Learning Based Approaches for Vapor–Liquid–Liquid Phase Equilibria in n-Octane/Water, as a Naphtha–Water Surrogate in Water Blends
    Application of machine learning (logistic regression, decision tree, k-neighbors, and support vector classification) to predict phase behavior of n-octane/water blends
  • 2020 - A Mid-Infrared Region (MIR) lumped Group Contribution based method for monitoring light gases and gasolines in Fluid Catalytic Cracking
    Application of nonlinear multivariable minimization to MIR signals
  • 2018 - Monitoring the progress of catalytic cracking for model compounds in the mid-infrared (MIR) 3200–2800 cm−1 range
    Data analysis of MIR spectroscopy signals to predict light absorption coefficient
  • 2015 - Stability criteria and critical runway conditions of propylene glycol manufacture in a continuous stirred tank reactor
    Parametric sensitivity analysis for the analysis of a steady state and safety operational conditions
  • 2015 - Transition metal loaded TiO2 for phenol photo-degradation

  • 2014 - Phenol Photo-Degradation over TiO2 and Mo/TiO2 Catalysts. The Response Surface Methodology as Optimization Tool

  • 2013 - Pervaporation membrane reactor design guidelines for the production of methyl acetate
