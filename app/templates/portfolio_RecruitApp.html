Recruit WebApp (Oct 2019 - Aug 2020)

Demo

Objective

Development of an API to improve the recruitment/training process of a local school bus company.

Business Problem:

The company was using a MS Access application to track the progress of an applicant through to licensing and offer of employment for almost 10 years. After this period, different inefficiencies emerged as:

  • The need of a portable solution available on tablets
  • The need to move organization to paperless in its recruitment/training process
  • The administrators required more flexibility in managing some of the data tables
  • The organization required evidence (for audit purposes) that training modules are executed according to guidelines

There was an opportunity to redesign and rebuild Recruit Tracker as an web application that is easily accessed on any device through a URL. This new application has the ability to:

  • Generate reports in CSV or PDF files, which can then be uploaded to other systems used within the company
  • Users have persions and access according to their roles improving accountability
  • Controls to ensure the users follow the processes were implemented to enforce the sequential delivery of training modules in the required amount of time.

Tools

  • Flask (Python)
  • SQL + SQLAlchemy
  • Pandas
  • HTML, CSS, JQuery
  • Pythonanywhere

Achievements

  • Migration of the recruitment process and data base from Access to a Web Application
  • Improvement traceability on the recruitmen and training processes implementing KPI
  • Integration of SQL database to flask web application
  • Deployment of API in Pythonanywhere and company servers

Multiple users login with different roles

Dashboard helps stakeholders follow the KPI relevant for the recruitment/training process

Applicants are organized in a table that briefly summarizes their information

Applicants profile gives detailed information on applicant status

Information required by the company can be collected and saved

Training time is controlled by a timer. Minimum time requirements are followed.

Reports are customized to business needs

Reports can be download specifying date range in different formats

Administrative tools to delete applicants, change user permisions, among others, are available
