SmartSito (Feb - May 2020)

Blog Github Presentation

Summary

Our team, "Smartsito", participated in the MS Discover AI Challenge: Smarter and Sustainable Economies ran by Agorize . As part of the challenge we ideated and developed a business plan, marketing strategy and a prototype to help reduce food waste. We got to the finale of the competition being among the Top 3 teams of the retail & E-commerce category. I wrote a blog post related to this challenge.

Objective

To ideate and develop a business plan, marketing strategy and a prototype to participate in the 2020 Microsoft Discover AI Challenge: Smarter and Sustainable Economies.

Business Problem

Food waste is a big problem in the world today:

  • More than 33% of food is wasted globally
  • More than 50% of food production is wasted in Canada costing $17 billion dollars per year
  • 47% of the waste come from househould

Business Opportunity

The opportunity is to create app that:

  • Allows consumers to use their smartphones to scan purchases receipts and keep track of the expiring date of the items they buy in one simple step
  • Sent notifications of the items that are about to expire and provided recipes and ideas to the consumer on how to use the items
  • incent the use of the platform and sharing data by a reward program in partnership with retailers
  • Help retailers to plan better inventories and reduce waste

Tools

  • Flask (Python)
  • Pandas
  • Azure Cognitive Services
  • Azure Websites

Achievements

  • We got to the finale of the competition: Top 3 in the Retail & E-commerce category (237 initial teams in 4 categories)
  • We proposed a solution for smart food waste leveraging AI and Analytics to create smarter and sustainable economies
  • We pitched our idea in front of specilized jury in business and AI

Your tool to smartly reduce your food waste!

Value for consumers: Education and awareness of food waste reduction at home

Value for retailers: Opportunity to get detailed insights
