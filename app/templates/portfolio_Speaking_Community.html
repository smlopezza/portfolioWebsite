Public Speaking & Community Engagement

Panels · Workshops · Mentorship

Summary

I use public speaking as a way to demystify AI, empower women and newcomers in AI & tech, and create more inclusive pathways into data, AI and technology.

As a Senior Data Scientist at Scotiabank and London’s Women in AI Canada Chapter Lead, I speak at conferences, universities, community events, and industry panels, sharing practical insights on AI, career development, and navigating the Canadian job market.
