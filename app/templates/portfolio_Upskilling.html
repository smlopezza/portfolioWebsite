Microsoft AI Upskilling Journey (May – August 2020)

Sprint 6 Sprint 5 Sprint 2 Agorize

Objective

Our team "Lopez AI" participated in the Microsoft Discover AI Upskilling journey. We developed different projects involving machine learning and artificial intelligence tools from Microsoft Azure Cloud services.

  • Sprint 6 - AI in Production & Responsible AI: (Winners) - We proposed to use Azure Computer Vision service to identify plates of cars with driving restrictions, helping authorities to ensure the compliance of "Pico y Placa" legislation.
  • Sprint 5 - Language & Speech in AI: We developed a chatbot to help increase awareness in Down Syndrome
  • Sprint 2 - Microsoft Azure & Earth wildlife: (2nd place + audience award) - We used a pretrained wildlife detection model on Azure to detect animals in camera traps photographies

Tools

  • Azure Cognitive Services
  • Microsoft Azure: Cloud Computing Services
  • Azure Websites
